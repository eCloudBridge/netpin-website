✦ Deploy Gate

Stop Risky Releases Before Production

Every pipeline run is evaluated against your quality policies and the real state of the infrastructure it is about to change. The verdict goes straight back to your CI.

  • Pipeline quality

    Configuration, ordering, failures, logs and steps from GitHub Actions, GitLab CI and Jenkins

  • Real change risk

    Open drift, a deploy that would overwrite a hotfix, low IDI, blast radius, recent incidents and rollback history

  • Approved, blocked or manual review

    Reported as a commit status; overrides require a justification and are audited
