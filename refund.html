Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026
Last Updated: August 20, 2026
Version: 1.0

This Refund & Cancellation Policy (“Policy”) governs refunds, cancellations, credits, and payment-related adjustments for subscriptions and services provided by eCloudBridge Technologies Pvt. Ltd., operating the Netpin platform (“Netpin,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Policy applies to customers, organizations, and authorized users purchasing or subscribing to Netpin through the Netpin website, application, sales order, proposal, order form, or other authorized purchasing channel.

This Policy should be read together with Netpin’s Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, applicable Order Form, Master Services Agreement (“MSA”), Statement of Work (“SOW”), and any other written agreement applicable to the customer.

Where a separately executed MSA, Order Form, SOW, or enterprise agreement contains specific refund or cancellation provisions, those provisions will prevail over this Policy to the extent of any inconsistency.

1. General Refund Policy

Netpin provides cloud-based software and related digital services that are provisioned and made available electronically.

Unless expressly stated otherwise in a written agreement or required by applicable law:

  • Subscription fees are non-refundable once a subscription has been activated.
  • Cancellation of a subscription does not automatically entitle the customer to a refund of amounts already paid for the current billing period.
  • Customers who cancel a subscription will generally retain access to the applicable paid services until the end of the current billing period, unless the account is terminated earlier under the Terms of Service.
  • We do not provide refunds for unused time, unused seats, unused integrations, unused features, unused subscription capacity, or a customer's decision not to use the service after purchase.

2. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may cancel their Netpin subscription at any time through the applicable account/billing settings or by contacting Netpin support.

Cancellation will:

  • Stop future automatic renewals.
  • Not normally refund the current billing period.
  • Not eliminate amounts already invoiced or contractually committed.
  • Not affect amounts due for services already provided.
  • Generally allow continued access until the end of the paid billing period.

For example, if a customer purchases a monthly subscription on January 10 and cancels on January 20, the subscription will normally remain active until February 9 and no refund will be issued for the unused portion of January.

3. Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are purchased for a defined annual commitment.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • Annual subscription fees are non-refundable after activation.
  • Cancellation will prevent renewal for the following subscription term.
  • Cancellation does not create an entitlement to a pro-rata refund for unused months.
  • Any committed minimum fees under an Order Form or MSA remain payable.
  • Promotional discounts or annual-plan discounts may not be recalculated after cancellation.

For enterprise customers, the applicable Order Form or MSA will govern the commercial commitment and termination rights.

4. Free Trials

Where Netpin offers a free trial, the trial is provided to allow customers to evaluate the platform before purchasing.

Unless otherwise specified for a particular promotion:

  • No refund is available for a paid subscription merely because the customer did not fully use the free trial.
  • Customers are responsible for cancelling before the trial converts into a paid subscription where automatic conversion has been disclosed.
  • Trial eligibility may be limited to one trial per organization or customer.
  • Netpin may terminate or restrict trials where there is suspected abuse, fraud, automated account creation, or violation of the Terms of Service.

If Netpin explicitly advertises a specific trial or money-back guarantee, the terms of that offer will apply.

5. Eligible Refund Circumstances

Although subscription fees are generally non-refundable, Netpin may approve a refund in the following circumstances.

5.1 Duplicate Payment

A refund may be issued where the customer has been charged more than once for the same subscription, invoice, or transaction due to a technical or billing error. The duplicate amount will normally be refunded after verification.

5.2 Incorrect Charge

A refund or billing adjustment may be issued where Netpin incorrectly charged:

  • An amount different from the agreed price;
  • A subscription that was not authorized;
  • A quantity materially different from the purchased quantity; or
  • A fee that was incorrectly applied due to a Netpin billing error.

5.3 Subscription Activation Failure

A refund may be issued where:

  • Payment was successfully completed;
  • The subscription could not be activated due to a Netpin-side technical failure; and
  • Netpin is unable to resolve the activation issue within a commercially reasonable period.

5.4 Material Service Failure

If Netpin experiences a prolonged and material failure that prevents the customer from accessing the core subscribed service, Netpin may, at its discretion:

  • Restore the service;
  • Provide service credits;
  • Extend the subscription period; or
  • Issue a partial or full refund where appropriate.

Refunds under this section will be evaluated based on the severity, duration, cause, and impact of the service failure. Routine maintenance, scheduled maintenance, temporary outages, third-party failures, internet connectivity problems, customer-side infrastructure issues, and events outside Netpin's reasonable control generally do not qualify for refunds.

5.5 Legal Requirement

Nothing in this Policy limits or excludes any refund, cancellation, or other right that cannot legally be excluded or waived under applicable law.

6. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will generally not be provided for:

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to use the service.
  • Failure to configure integrations.
  • Failure to complete onboarding.
  • Lack of internal resources to use Netpin.
  • Employee turnover.
  • Change in business priorities.
  • Customer choosing not to deploy or continue using Netpin.
  • Unused subscription time.
  • Unused seats or capacity.
  • Unused credits, unless otherwise stated.
  • Failure to achieve a particular business result that was not expressly guaranteed in writing.
  • Customer-side configuration errors.
  • Customer infrastructure failures.
  • Third-party cloud provider outages.
  • Internet or connectivity problems outside Netpin's control.
  • Suspension caused by customer violation of Netpin's Terms of Service.
  • Fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Accounts terminated because of security, compliance, or acceptable-use violations.

7. Professional Services, Implementation and Consulting Fees

Where Netpin or its affiliates provide implementation, onboarding, consulting, migration, architecture, integration, training, assessment, or other professional services:

  • Fees for services already performed are non-refundable.
  • If a project is cancelled before completion, any refund or credit will be determined based on:
    • Work already completed;
    • Resources allocated;
    • Third-party expenses;
    • Deliverables already provided;
    • Project stage;
    • Non-cancellable commitments; and
    • The applicable SOW or Order Form.

Where a signed SOW contains specific cancellation or termination terms, those terms will control.

8. Paid Pilots and Proof-of-Concepts

Paid pilots, proof-of-concepts (“POCs”), assessments, and evaluation engagements are generally non-refundable once the agreed services have commenced.

Before a paid pilot begins, Netpin and the customer may agree in writing on: Pilot duration, Scope, Success criteria, Deliverables, Fees, Data requirements, Cancellation terms, and Conversion terms.

If the pilot cannot commence due to a Netpin-side failure, Netpin may provide a refund, credit, or alternative remedy at its discretion.

A pilot does not constitute a guarantee that the customer will achieve a particular operational, financial, security, or performance outcome unless explicitly stated in the applicable agreement.

9. Promotional Credits and Coupons

Promotional credits, coupons, discounts, free credits, referral credits, and other promotional benefits:

  • Have no cash value;
  • Are non-transferable;
  • Cannot normally be exchanged for a refund;
  • May have an expiration date;
  • May be subject to additional promotional terms.

If an account is refunded, promotional credits used toward that purchase may be cancelled or deducted from the refundable amount where permitted.

10. Refund Request Process

Customers requesting a refund should contact Netpin through the official support or billing channel.

A refund request should include:

  • Customer/company name;
  • Registered account email;
  • Invoice number;
  • Transaction/reference ID;
  • Date of payment;
  • Amount paid;
  • Reason for the refund request; and
  • Supporting information, where applicable.

Netpin may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the request.

Refund requests should generally be submitted within 30 days of the relevant transaction or billing event, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or expressly provided in an applicable agreement.

Submitting a request does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

11. Refund Review

Netpin will review eligible refund requests based on: Applicable subscription terms, Billing records, Account activity, Service availability, Usage, Contractual commitments, The nature of the reported issue, Applicable Order Forms/MSAs/SOWs, and Applicable law.

Netpin may approve:

  • Full refund;
  • Partial refund;
  • Service credit;
  • Subscription extension;
  • Billing correction; or
  • No refund.

Where appropriate, Netpin will communicate the decision and the basis for it.

12. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be processed using the original payment method.

Where payments are processed through a third-party payment provider such as Razorpay, the refund will be initiated through the applicable payment provider. The time required for the funds to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider, card network, bank, or other financial institution.

Netpin does not control the processing time after the refund has been successfully initiated. For payments processed through Razorpay, refund processing timelines may vary by payment method and banking institution. Razorpay documents normal refunds as generally taking approximately 5–7 working days.

13. Taxes

Where applicable, refunds will be processed subject to applicable tax laws.

If taxes have already been deposited with the relevant government authority or cannot legally be reversed, the refundable amount may be adjusted accordingly. Any applicable GST or other tax treatment will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

14. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact Netpin before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute.

If a customer initiates a chargeback while a refund request is under review, Netpin may suspend the refund review until the payment dispute is resolved.

Where Netpin determines that a chargeback is fraudulent, abusive, or inconsistent with the customer's contractual obligations, Netpin reserves the right to provide relevant transaction and account information to the payment processor or financial institution as permitted by law.

Nothing in this section limits any rights the customer may have under applicable law or payment-network rules.

15. Account Termination

If an account is terminated because of: Fraud, Abuse, Security violations, Unauthorized access, Misuse of the platform, Violation of the Terms of Service, Violation of applicable law, or Other material contractual violations, the customer will generally not be entitled to a refund of amounts already paid.

Termination does not release the customer from amounts already due or payable under an applicable agreement.

16. Service Credits

Where appropriate, Netpin may provide service credits instead of monetary refunds.

Service credits may be applied toward future subscription invoices and:

  • Have no cash value;
  • Cannot normally be transferred;
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash;
  • May expire according to the terms under which they were issued.

Where an enterprise agreement contains a service-level agreement (“SLA”), the SLA's service-credit provisions will apply.

17. Enterprise Customers

For enterprise customers, the applicable: Master Services Agreement, Order Form, Subscription Agreement, Statement of Work, Service Level Agreement, or Other executed commercial agreement may contain different refund, termination, cancellation, service-credit, or payment provisions.

In the event of a conflict, the separately executed agreement will prevail over this Policy to the extent of that conflict.

18. International Customers

Netpin may provide services to customers located outside India. International customers may be subject to additional payment, tax, currency conversion, regulatory, and consumer-protection requirements.

Nothing in this Policy is intended to remove or restrict any mandatory right available to a customer under applicable law. Where legally required, Netpin will comply with applicable refund, cancellation, or consumer-protection requirements.

19. Policy Against Abuse

Netpin reserves the right to investigate refund requests where there is evidence of: Refund abuse, Multiple accounts created to obtain repeated refunds or trials, Fraud, Unauthorized use, Manipulation of billing, Payment fraud, Account sharing in violation of the Terms of Service, or Other misuse.

Netpin may deny refund requests and/or suspend accounts where permitted by its Terms of Service and applicable law.

20. Changes to This Policy

Netpin may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published through the applicable Netpin website or platform.

Changes will generally apply prospectively and will not retroactively remove rights that have already accrued under an existing agreement or applicable law.
