✦ About Netpin

The Story of Netpin:
Bringing Order to the Chaos

Infrastructure used to be a black box of hidden costs and sudden failures. We built Netpin because engineering teams deserve complete visibility. We exist to make Technical Debt measurable and turn abstract complexity into clear, actionable intelligence.

Our Mission

Infrastructure complexity is growing faster than teams can manage it. Security vulnerabilities, compliance gaps, and operational inefficiencies accumulate silently until they cause outages, breaches, or burnout.

We built Netpin to change that. By quantifying infrastructure debt into a single, actionable score — the Infrastructure Debt Index — we give teams a common language and clear priorities.

No more guessing. No more firefighting. Just data-driven decisions that keep your infrastructure healthy and your team productive.
