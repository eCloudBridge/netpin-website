Automating Compliance: SOC 2 & CIS for Kubernetes

Aug 27, 2026 6 min read Netpin Engineering

The Audit Scramble

For years, compliance meant a frantic two-week scramble of taking screenshots of AWS consoles and Kubernetes terminal outputs. With Netpin's Compliance Engine, this is entirely automated. By mapping CIS, SOC 2, PCI DSS, and NIST controls directly against your real-time cluster inventory, Netpin ensures your infrastructure is always audit-ready.

Continuous Control Monitoring

Once you connect your cloud providers, Netpin continuously evaluates your environment against standard frameworks. Any deviation generates a finding with a detailed "Explain & Fix" packet, complete with Terraform or kubectl remediation commands. No more guesswork, no more audit anxiety.
