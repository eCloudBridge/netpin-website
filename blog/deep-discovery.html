You Can't Secure What You Can't See

Shadow IT and unmanaged resources are the plague of modern multi-cloud environments. The first step to reducing your Infrastructure Debt Index (IDI) is mapping out exactly what you have. This is where Netpin's Deep Discovery comes in.

Blast Radius Analysis

By visualizing your infrastructure topology down to the node level, Deep Discovery calculates the Blast Radius of potential changes. Before you deploy, you can see the impact neighborhood graph, enabling SREs and Platform Engineers to make safe, confident rollout decisions.
