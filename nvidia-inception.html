✦ Transparency & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding Netpin's participation in the NVIDIA Inception Program.

What is the NVIDIA Inception Program?

NVIDIA Inception is a free, non-equity startup acceleration program designed to nurture startups revolutionizing industries with artificial intelligence and accelerated computing. It provides members with technical guidance, developer tools, hardware credits, and go-to-market opportunities.

Does membership mean NVIDIA endorses or certifies Netpin?

No. Participation in the NVIDIA Inception Program is a factual alignment within NVIDIA's startup developer ecosystem. It does not constitute a formal certification, corporate endorsement, product warranty, or equity investment by NVIDIA Corporation.

Do Netpin customers need NVIDIA GPUs in their clusters to use Netpin?

No. Netpin works seamlessly on any standard Kubernetes cluster (EKS, GKE, AKS, DOKS, or self-hosted) and cloud environment without requiring GPU hardware on the customer side. The Netpin agent is a lightweight, read-only daemon requiring minimal CPU and RAM. Accelerated compute and AI model pipelines operate within Netpin's cloud infrastructure.

Is customer infrastructure data shared with NVIDIA?

No. Customer telemetry and metadata are strictly governed by Netpin's zero-trust security framework and Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Netpin only analyzes infrastructure configuration metadata; application payloads and customer data are never stored or shared with external parties.
